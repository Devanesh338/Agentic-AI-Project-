## 📅 PROPOSED ITINERARY  
**Length:** 6 days / 5 nights (arrive Day 0, depart Day 6) – the sweet spot for seeing the highlights without feeling rushed.  

> **Why 6 days?**  
> - Gives you a full arrival day to recover from the long flight.  
> - Allows two full “culture” days (Asakusa, Ueno, Meiji‑Shibuya).  
> - One day for a classic day‑trip (Nikko or Hakone).  
> - One “free‑flow” day to explore neighborhoods you love most (e.g., Akihabara, Daikanyama, or a shopping spree).  

> **Weather note (mid‑Sept 2026):** Light‑to‑moderate rain is forecast for the first 24 h after arrival. Pack a compact **travel umbrella** and waterproof shoes; most major attractions are indoor or have covered walkways, so the rain won’t ruin the plan.
