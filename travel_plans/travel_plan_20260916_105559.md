## 6️⃣ Practical Tips – Making the ₹10 k Work

| Topic | Tip |

| **Baggage** | Travel with a **7 kg cabin bag** only. If you need a larger bag, pre‑pay the **₹500** checked‑bag fee online (cheaper than airport price). |
| **Accommodation** | Choose **Treebo Trend** or **Zostel** – they both include **breakfast** and free Wi‑Fi, saving ~₹250/day on food. |
| **Metro** | Buy a **₹500 Smart Card** (₹100 deposit + ₹400 credit). You’ll get a 10 % discount on each ride vs single‑ticket. |
| **Food** | Eat at **local “mess” style eateries** (MTR, Vidyarthi Bhavan) for ₹80‑₹200 per meal. Avoid tourist‑trap restaurants on MG Road for lunch. |
| **Sight‑seeing** | Most museums have **student/​senior concessions** (₹20‑₹30). Carry a **photo ID**. |
| **Cash vs Card** | Use **UPI/Google Pay** for most transactions (no extra fees). Keep **₹2 000 cash** for auto‑rickshaws and small stalls. |
| **Weather (late‑Sept)** | Expect **28‑30 °C** with a chance of light evening rain. Carry a **compact umbrella** (₹120) and stay hydrated. |
| **Safety** | Bangalore is generally safe, but **keep your bags close** on crowded metros/buses. Use **registered auto‑rickshaws** (look for “Metered” sign). |
| **Connectivity** | Free Wi‑Fi at most cafés and the **airport lounge**. If you need constant data, buy a **daily data pack** from Jio/​Airtel (₹150). |
