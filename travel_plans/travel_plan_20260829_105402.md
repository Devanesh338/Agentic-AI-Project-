## **Day 1: Arrival & Colonial Delhi**

**Theme:** Acclimatization & British Era Architecture

*   **08:00 AM:** Arrive at Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL).
*   **09:30 AM:** Check into your hotel (drop off luggage if not ready).
*   **10:30 AM:** **Breakfast** at the hotel or a nearby café.
*   **12:00 PM:** **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (Presidential Palace) – View from outside.
*   **02:00 PM:** **India Gate** – Monument dedicated to soldiers who died in WWI. Great for photos and people-watching.
*   **04:00 PM:** **Lunch** at **Kamakshi Bhavan** (South Indian food, close to CP) or **Tandoori Palace** (North Indian).
*   **05:30 PM:** **Connaught Place (CP)** – Evening stroll through the circular market. Browse bookstores (Pearl Book Centre), jewelry shops, and cafes.
*   **08:00 PM:** **Dinner** at **The Grand Café** or **Barbeque Nation** (CP).
*   **09:30 PM:** Return to hotel. Rest.

**Cost Estimate (Day 1):**
*   Local Transport (Metro/Taxi): ₹1,500
*   Food: ₹1,500
*   Entry Fees: ₹0 (India Gate is free)

---

## **Day 2: Old Delhi & Mughal Heritage**

**Theme:** Historical Sights & Street Food

*   **08:00 AM:** **Breakfast** at hotel.
*   **09:30 AM:** Take a pre-booked auto-rickshaw or taxi to **Old Delhi**.
*   **10:30 AM:** **Red Fort (Lal Qila)** – UNESCO World Heritage Site. Enter from Lahori Gate. Allow 2 hours.
    *   *Tip:* Book tickets online to avoid long queues.
*   **01:00 PM:** **Lunch** at **Paranthe Wali Gali** (famous for stuffed parathas) or **Karahi Kebab** (near Jama Masjid).
*   **03:00 PM:** **Jama Masjid** – One of the largest mosques in India.
    *   *Note:* Non-Muslims cannot enter the main prayer hall but can view the courtyard. Dress modestly (cover shoulders and knees).
*   **04:30 PM:** **Chandni Chowk** – Explore the bustling old market. Try street food:
    *   **Chandni Chowk Chaat**
    *   **Gali Ka Baap** (Parathas)
    *   **Paranthe Wali Gali** (if not tried earlier)
*   **06:30 PM:** Return to hotel for a break.
*   **08:00 PM:** **Dinner** at **Sarai Kalan** or **Kunal** (North Indian classics).
*   **10:00 PM:** Optional: Evening rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk (if you didn’t do it earlier).

**Cost Estimate (Day 2):**
*   Local Transport: ₹1,000
*   Food: ₹1,500
*   Entry Fees: Red Fort (₹500 for foreigners, ₹100 for Indians)

---

## **Day 3: Mughal Gardens & Modern Delhi**

**Theme:** Architecture, Peace & Shopping

*   **08:30 AM:** **Breakfast** at hotel.
*   **10:00 AM:** **Humayun’s Tomb** – UNESCO World Heritage Site, precursor to the Taj Mahal. Beautiful gardens and architecture. Allow 1.5 hours.
*   **12:30 PM:** **Qutub Minar** – 73m tall tower, tallest brick minaret in the world.
    *   *Tip:* Combine with **Qutub Complex** (Alai Darwaza, Iron Pillar).
*   **02:00 PM:** **Lunch** at **Moti Mahal** (Daryaganj) – Famous for Mughlai cuisine (Butter Chicken, Dal Makhani).
*   **04:00 PM:** **Lotus Temple** – Bahá’í House of Worship. Serene architecture.
    *   *Note:* Closed on Tuesdays. Dress modestly. No photography inside.
*   **06:00 PM:** **Sarojini Nagar** or **Saket** – Leisure shopping.
    *   **Sarojini Nagar:** Best for fashion, accessories, and bargains.
    *   **Saket:** More upscale, good for electronics and branded stores.
*   **08:30 PM:** **Dinner** at **Sharma’s Tandoor** (Saket) or **Kebabs by Saad** (Saket).
*   **10:30 PM:** Return to hotel.

**Cost Estimate (Day 3):**
*   Local Transport: ₹1,200
*   Food: ₹2,000
*   Entry Fees: Humayun’s Tomb (₹150), Qutub Minar (₹100)

---

**Theme:** Relaxation & Return Journey

*   **09:00 AM:** **Breakfast** at hotel.
*   **10:30 AM:** Check out of hotel. Keep luggage at hotel front desk.

    *   *Tip:* Allow 3 hours for check-in, security, and immigration.
*   **08:00 PM:** Depart for Chennai (MAA).
*   **10:30 PM:** Arrive in Chennai.
